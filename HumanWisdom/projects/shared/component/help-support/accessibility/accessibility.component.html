Accessibility Policy

Accessibility Statement for HappierMe

At HappierMe, we are committed to making our wellness app accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We believe that emotional wellbeing tools should be inclusive, easy to use, and supportive for all users, regardless of ability.

Our Commitment

We strive to ensure that HappierMe is accessible and usable in accordance with recognized accessibility standards, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

Accessibility Features

The HappierMe app currently supports the following:

  • Compatibility with screen readers
  • High-contrast interface for better readability
  • Clear, simple navigation and design
  • Videos with subtitles
  • Both text and audio formats in key areas
  • Support for larger font sizes

Continuous Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We are actively working to improve the usability of our app and expand our support for assistive technologies. Your feedback plays an important role in this process.

Feedback & Contact

If you experience any difficulty accessing content or features in HappierMe, or if you have suggestions for improvement, we’d love to hear from you.

Email: support@happierme.app
Subject line: Accessibility Feedback

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 3 working days.
