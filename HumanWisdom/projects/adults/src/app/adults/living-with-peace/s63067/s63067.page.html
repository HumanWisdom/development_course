#6 What can my emotional pain teach me about myself?

 

 

The body
THE ‘I’
A physical pain in my body calls my attention to an issue that I need to address. E.g. a toothache
An emotional pain calls my attention to an issue inside me. I need to travel inwards and understand the root cause of it in my thinking. If I ignore it or blame others, it never goes away.
