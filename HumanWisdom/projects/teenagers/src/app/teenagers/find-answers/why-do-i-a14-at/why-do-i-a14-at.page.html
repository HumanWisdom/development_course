Feel the pressure to conform

At different times in our life we have all felt the pressure to conform to what others were thinking or doing, so you are not alone if you feel this way. It is magnified many times over by social media.

In this podcast we are going to explore how it makes us feel and behave, what is going on in our mind to make us feel this way and what we can do about it.

It starts in the playground as children. One person wants to play a certain game and wants others to join in, and over rules others who want to do something different. The person who is most forceful usually gets their way and the rest follow suit. This can continue when you are a teenager but the decisions may be about which movie to go and see or whom to include or exclude from the group.

With social media the pressure can be much more subtle and you may not even be aware of why you suddenly want to dress in a certain way or shape your eyebrows, or conform to a certain body shape. Our minds are easily conditioned by our many influences and this conditioning then determines our thoughts and actions. It's not wrong but happens without our awareness. By asking ourselves a simple question - why am I thinking or acting in this way, we can become aware of our conditioning and then decide whether it serves us.

Our need to fit in and the fear of being excluded from the group can also push us to conform to the norms of the group and make us behave in ways that make us feel uncomfortable - for example teasing someone the group does not like, or worse.

The key thing to realise is that all the pressures you feel to conform may not be real, and you can decide to opt out and be your own person. That needs the courage.
